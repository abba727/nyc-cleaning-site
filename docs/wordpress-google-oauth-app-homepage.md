# NYC Cleaning

## Administrator Content Management Application

The **NYC Cleaning** application is a private website content-management system for authorized administrators of NYC Cleaning and Maintenance Group, LLC. It allows approved team members to securely sign in and manage the articles, images, and other published content displayed on the NYC Cleaning website.

### What the application does

Authorized administrators use the application to create, edit, publish, and remove website content; upload and manage images associated with that content; and maintain accurate information on the public NYC Cleaning website. The application is an internal administrative tool and is not offered to the general public.

### How Google Sign-In is used

The application uses **Google Sign-In solely for authentication and access control**. When an approved administrator signs in, the application receives basic Google Account information: the user’s name, email address, profile image, and unique Google account identifier. This information is used only to verify the user’s identity, confirm that the user has been authorized to access the administrator application, maintain the user’s secure session, and display basic account information inside the application.

The application does **not** request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, or other Google Workspace content. It does not post content to a user’s Google account.

### Who can access the application

Access is restricted to NYC Cleaning administrators who have been approved or invited by the primary administrator. Possession of a Google Account does not automatically grant access. Users who are not on the approved administrator list cannot enter the content-management system.

## WordPress Publishing Instructions

Publish this as a **normal public WordPress page**. Do not password-protect it, hide it behind a sign-in screen, mark it `noindex`, or require cookies or form submission to read it. Use the standard NYC Cleaning header, logo, and footer.

Place the page in the main navigation or footer with a visible link labeled **Administrator Application**. Add visible **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service** links to the global footer as well. The two legal documents must have separate public URLs.

On the Google OAuth consent-screen Branding page, set **Application home page** to this dedicated WordPress page. Keep the app name exactly **NYC Cleaning** on the consent screen, because that exact name appears as the first heading on this page and matches the business branding.

Replace both bracketed policy URL placeholders before publishing. In WordPress, make each policy name a normal clickable text link rather than showing only plain URL text.
