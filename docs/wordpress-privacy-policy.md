# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 14, 2026

NYC Cleaning and Maintenance Group, LLC (referred to as **“NYC Cleaning,” “we,” “us,”** or **“our”**) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect information when you visit **https://www.nyccleaning.co**, submit a request through our website, or use the private NYC Cleaning website administration and content-management application (collectively, the **“Services”**).

## 1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through our public website and our private website administration application. The administration application allows authorized NYC Cleaning personnel and invited administrators to sign in, manage website content, and perform related administrative functions.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites or services that we do not control. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly

When you contact us, request a quote, or submit another website form, we may collect information such as:

* Your name;
* Your email address;
* Your telephone number;
* The service in which you are interested;
* Property, project, or service-request details;
* The contents of your message; and
* Any other information you choose to provide.

When an authorized administrator manages website content, we may also process content, article drafts, images, publishing information, and administrative activity associated with that user.

### Information Received Through Google Sign-In

The private administration application uses Google Sign-In to authenticate authorized administrators. When you choose to sign in with Google, we may receive the following information from Google, depending on the permissions displayed on Google’s consent screen:

* Your Google Account email address;
* Your name;
* Your Google Account profile image, if available; and
* A unique Google Account identifier used to distinguish your account securely.

We use this Google Account information only to authenticate you, confirm that your email address is authorized to access the administration application, display basic account information, maintain your signed-in session, protect the application, and keep appropriate administrative records.

The application is designed to request only the basic **OpenID**, **email**, and **profile** scopes needed for sign-in. It does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, passwords, or payment information.

NYC Cleaning does not sell Google user data. We do not use Google user data for advertising, credit decisions, or unrelated profiling. We do not allow humans to read Google user data except when necessary for security, support, compliance with law, or at the user’s request. We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as described in this Privacy Policy, as necessary to provide or secure the Services, with the user’s consent, or as required by law.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the **Google API Services User Data Policy**, including its Limited Use requirements.

### Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Services, our systems and service providers may automatically collect limited technical and usage information, such as:

* Internet Protocol address;
* Browser type and device type;
* Operating system;
* Referring and requested pages;
* Date and time of access;
* Approximate location derived from an IP address;
* Error, security, and diagnostic information; and
* Cookie and session information.

We use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Services; understand general website usage; detect abuse; and maintain reliable performance.

## 3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies that are necessary to operate the website and administration application. For example, the administration application uses a secure session cookie to keep an authorized administrator signed in and to protect restricted areas.

Your browser may allow you to block or delete cookies. Blocking essential cookies may prevent sign-in or other parts of the Services from functioning correctly.

## 4. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

* Respond to questions, quote requests, and service inquiries;
* Communicate about requested or contracted services;
* Authenticate and authorize administrators;
* Send and manage administrator invitations;
* Operate the website and content-management functions;
* Maintain security and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access;
* Diagnose technical problems and improve the Services;
* Maintain business, compliance, and administrative records;
* Enforce our agreements and policies; and
* Comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 5. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information in the following circumstances:

### Service Providers

We may share information with vendors that perform services for us, such as website hosting, cloud infrastructure, database hosting, email delivery, security, analytics, and technical support. These providers are permitted to process information only for the services they provide to us and subject to appropriate contractual or legal obligations.

Administrator invitation emails may be delivered through **Resend**. Google provides the identity authentication service used by the private administration application.

### Legal, Safety, and Business Purposes

We may disclose information when we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to comply with law, legal process, or a government request; protect the rights, property, or safety of NYC Cleaning, our users, or others; investigate fraud or security incidents; enforce our agreements; or support a merger, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, subject to applicable law.

### With Your Direction or Consent

We may disclose information when you direct us to do so or otherwise provide consent.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing the Services, maintaining administrative and business records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, meeting legal obligations, and protecting the security and integrity of the Services.

Google profile information used for administrator access is retained while the account remains authorized and as reasonably necessary for security and administrative records. If administrator access is removed, we may retain limited records where necessary for security, auditing, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

## 7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include restricted administrative access, encrypted network transmission, protected server-side credentials, and secure session controls where appropriate.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, such as the right to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To submit a privacy request, email **info@nyccleaning.co**. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Certain information may be retained where required or permitted by law.

### Revoking Google Account Access

You may review or revoke the application’s access to your Google Account through your Google Account’s third-party connections or permissions settings at **https://myaccount.google.com/connections**. Revoking Google access may sign you out or prevent you from using the private administration application.

Authorized administrators may also request removal of their NYC Cleaning administration access by contacting **info@nyccleaning.co**.

## 9. Children’s Privacy

The Services are intended for businesses, property professionals, adults seeking cleaning or maintenance services, and authorized website administrators. They are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the Services. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

## 10. Third-Party Links and Services

The website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy, security, or content practices of third parties. We encourage you to review their policies before providing information.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we make changes, we will revise the “Last Updated” date above and publish the updated policy on this page. Material changes may be communicated through the website, the administration application, or another reasonable method.
